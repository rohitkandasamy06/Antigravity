Problem Formulation

Industry Problem: Automotive Aftermarket Inventory Volatility

Automotive supply chains manage over 100,000+ SKUs across fluctuating regional hubs.

Stockout Downtime
A single missing turbo gasket or brake pad halts commercial fleets, fleet contracts, and dealership service bays, causing heavy revenue leakage.
Holding Capital Obsolescence
Holding excess winter stock or slow-moving performance inventory freezes working capital across regional distribution hubs.
Static ERP Blindness
Traditional ERP dashboards only display historical data without non-linear predictive forecasting or natural language operational intelligence.
System Architecture

Proposed Solution: Dual-Tier AI Intelligence

Bridging deterministic numerical Supervised ML with conversational Generative AI.

• Technique: In-Context Grounding with live warehouse KPIs

Mathematical Modeling

Domain Feature Engineering & Elasticity

Capturing non-linear economic and temporal dynamics driving auto parts velocity.

Non-Linear Demand Equation
Demand = (Base × CatMult × HubMult × SeasonIndex × DiscountBoost × LeadTimeFactor × WeekendBoost) / PriceRatio
• Seasonal Index: Sinusoidal cycle peaking during Spring Tuning Season (Mar–May) and Pre-Winter fleet maintenance.
• Competitor Index: Relative ratio dynamically shifting orders based on competitor pricing.
Temporal Autocorrelation Lags
• lag_1_units: Immediate prior day order volume.
• lag_7_units: Captures weekly shop replenishment cycles.
• lag_14_units: Bi-weekly distributor ordering cycles.
• rolling_mean_7d: 7-day trend smoothing moving average.
• rolling_std_7d: 7-day demand volatility & risk index.
Validation Integrity

Chronological Split: Zero Lookahead Leakage

Standard K-Fold cross-validation is invalid for time-series. We enforced strict temporal partitioning.

Used to compute tree splits, residual gradients, and feature split gains.

Guarantees unbiased generalization score on future calendar quarters.
